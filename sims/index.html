    Signal Processing MicroSims

    Interactive Micro Simulations to help students learn signal processing fundamentals. Every MicroSim runs in your browser, uses p5.js 2.x, and has a lesson plan with activities and assessment questions.

    Signal Processing MicroSims

    • 2-D Convolution and Image Filtering

      Slide a 3×3 or 5×5 kernel over a small grayscale image and see every multiply-add: blur, sharpen, Sobel and Laplacian kernels, padding, flipping and noise.

    • A Convolutional Layer Is a Learned FIR Filter

      Train a tiny 1-D convolutional network in the browser and watch its kernels become band-pass FIR filters tuned to the tones it must tell apart.

    • Adaptive Algorithm Comparison Tool

      LMS, NLMS and RLS identify the same unknown FIR plant from the same noisy data. Compare convergence speed, steady-state error, stability and tracking.

    • Adaptive Noise and Echo Cancellation

      One NLMS adaptive filter, three jobs - noise cancellation with a reference input, acoustic echo cancellation with double-talk, and channel equalization.

    • Analog Modulation

      Compare amplitude modulation (AM) and frequency modulation (FM) of a sine carrier by a sine message, in time and as a line spectrum with its bandwidth.

    • Basic FFT MicroSim

      A sine-wave generator with its live FFT - the waveform in the time domain on top and its magnitude spectrum below, with frequency and amplitude sliders.

    • Bass and Treble Amplifier Controls

      Frequency response of an amplifier's bass and treble controls, drawn as gain in dB against log frequency, with two sliders and a mouse probe that reads the gain.

    • Bilinear Transform and Frequency Warping

      Compare the bilinear transform and impulse invariance - frequency warping, prewarping, aliasing near Nyquist and how s-plane poles map into the z-plane.

    • Citation Graph for FFT on Microcontrollers

      Explore a 15-paper citation graph of FFT research on microcontrollers. Filter by research area and citation count, then compare three layouts.

    • Complex Plane

      Interactive visualization of complex numbers in the complex plane, showing both rectangular (a + bi) and polar (r angle theta) forms with geometric representations.

    • Compressed Sensing

      Recover a sparse spectrum of k tones from m random time samples with orthogonal matching pursuit, and compare it with the minimum-energy solution and uniform sampling.

    • Convolution

      A blue square slides across a red triangle while a purple gauge shows the area of overlap, the introductory picture of convolution.

    • Convolution Theorem and Circular Convolution

      Compare linear convolution with L-point circular convolution computed as IDFT(DFT(x)·DFT(h)), and watch the tail wrap around when L < N + M − 1.

    • Convolution Visualizer

      Step through the flip-and-slide picture of convolution. The impulse response is time-reversed, shifted, multiplied by the input, and the area under the product builds the output y(t).

    • Correlation and Matched Filtering

      Find copies of a known template hidden in noise by cross-correlating the received signal with the template, and compare the matched filter with a simple moving average.

    • DCT Basis Explorer

      Explore the 8-point DCT-II basis functions and the 64 basis images of the 8x8 DCT, and see why energy compaction makes the DCT the core of JPEG.

    • DFT Explorer

      Build each DFT value X[k] by multiplying x[n] by a rotating phasor and adding the products head to tail, then rebuild x[n] from the bins you keep.

    • Euler's Formula Explorer

      Watch the point e^(iθ) travel around the unit circle while its real and imaginary parts trace the cosine and sine waves, with live numeric read-outs.

    • Even and Odd Signal Decomposition

      Decompose a preset or hand-drawn signal x(t) into its even part and odd part, read the worked calculation at a probe time, and verify that the two parts sum back to x(t).

    • FFT Butterfly

      Step through the input reordering of a radix-2 FFT, splitting every group into even and odd samples until they sit in bit-reversed order.

    • FFT Live Audio from Microphone

      A live spectrum analyzer for your microphone with adjustable smoothness and FFT size, showing bin width, window length and the strongest peak.

    • FFT of Sound File

      A music player with a track list that shows the waveform and the FFT frequency spectrum of the song you are listening to.

    • FFT Size MicroSim

      A sine wave analysed with FFT sizes from 512 to 8192 bins. Larger FFTs give narrower bins and peaks but need a longer analysis window.

    • FFT Spectrum Analyzer

      Analyze test signals or the microphone with a radix-2 FFT, choosing FFT size, window and a magnitude, dB or phase display, with peak markers and a spectrogram.

    • FFT Waveform Types

      Compare the harmonic spectra of sine, square, triangle and sawtooth waves - which harmonics each shape contains and how fast they decay.

    • FFT with Multiple Frequency Components

      Three sine-wave oscillators are added together - the combined waveform in the time domain and its FFT spectrum with one labelled peak per component.

    • FFT with Two Oscillators

      A sine and a cosine oscillator are added and played; see their sum in the time domain and one FFT peak per oscillator in the magnitude spectrum.

    • Filter Bank and Polyphase Explorer

      Split a signal into M subbands with a cosine-modulated filter bank, measure the reconstruction error, and see why polyphase decimation is M times cheaper.

    • FIR Filter Design Comparison Tool

      Compare two linear-phase FIR designs (window method, frequency sampling or Parks-McClellan) for the same specification, with measured ripple, attenuation and transition width.

    • FIR vs IIR Filters

      Compare an FIR windowed-sinc and an IIR Butterworth low-pass with the same cutoff - response, phase, delay, cost and stability under coefficient rounding.

    • Fourier Series Explorer

      Build square, triangle, sawtooth, pulse and hand-drawn waveforms from their first N harmonics; read each harmonic's amplitude, phase and the Gibbs overshoot.

    • Fourier Transform Pairs

      Explore five classic Fourier transform pairs and the time-scaling and time-shift properties, with each analytic X(f) checked against an FFT estimate.

    • Frequency Detection

      A winding machine wraps a sine wave around a spinning circle; the center of mass of the wound curve reveals the signal frequency, as the Fourier transform does.

    • Huffman Coding

      Build a Huffman tree one merge at a time, read off the prefix-free code, and compare the average code length L with the entropy H and a fixed-length code.

    • IIR Prototype Comparison

      Compare Butterworth, Chebyshev I and II, elliptic and Bessel low-pass prototypes at the same order - magnitude, phase, group delay, step response and poles.

    • Impulse and Frequency Response Analyzer

      See the impulse response, step response, magnitude response and phase response of five LTI systems side by side, with linked cursors and sliders for cutoff frequency, filter order and damping ratio.

    • Interactive Filter Design and Analysis Tool

      Design low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, band-stop, notch, comb and all-pass filters as IIR or FIR and see their responses, poles, stability and effect on three tones.

    • Interactive Pole-Zero Analysis Tool

      Place poles and zeros on the z-plane and watch the magnitude response, phase response, impulse response, step response and stability verdict of the discrete-time system update.

    • Interactive Spectrogram Parameter Explorer

      Compare two spectrograms of one signal or your microphone, each with its own window length, overlap, window, zero padding, dB floor and color map.

    • Interactive STFT Spectrogram Analyzer

      Compare short- and long-window STFT spectrograms of one test signal and use cursors to see what each window can and cannot separate in time and frequency.

    • Microphone Frequency Visualization with FFT

      A real-time spectrum analyzer for your microphone. An FFT turns live sound into colored frequency bars with a decibel scale and a peak-frequency read-out.

    • Multirate Sample Rate Converter

      Change a signal's sample rate by L/M and watch zero insertion create images, downsampling fold aliases, and the low-pass filter remove both.

    • Möbius Transform

      Drag the coefficients of w = (Az + B)/(Cz + D), draw in the z-plane and watch lines and circles map, including the bilinear transform used for IIR filters.

    • Noise Analysis and Reduction Tool

      Generate white, pink, brown, band-limited and impulsive noise, measure its histogram, autocorrelation and Welch PSD, and see SNR improve with averaging and filtering.

    • Nyquist-Shannon Sampling

      Sample a 5 Hz sine wave at 1 to 50 Hz and compare ideal sinc and straight-line reconstructions to see the Nyquist rate and aliasing at work.

    • Phase Spectrum - What the Magnitude Hides

      Same magnitude spectrum, different phases, different waveforms. Explore harmonic phases, the linear phase of a time shift, and the magnitude/phase swap experiment.

    • Phasor Addition

      Add up to three sinusoids by adding their rotating phasors head to tail, read the resultant amplitude and phase, and see beats when one frequency differs.

    • Power Spectral Density and the Wiener-Khinchin Theorem

      Estimate a PSD two ways, by averaged periodograms and by the Fourier transform of the sample autocorrelation, and compare both with the exact S(ω).

    • Probability Distribution Explorer

      Sample uniform, Gaussian, exponential, binomial and Poisson distributions, compare sample and true mean and variance, and watch the central limit theorem.

    • Quantization and Noise Shaping

      Quantize a full-scale waveform with 1 to 16 bits, see the quantization error and its spectrum, and measure how oversampling and noise shaping raise the SQNR in the signal band.

    • RC Low-Pass Filter Bode Plot

      Set R and C of an RC low-pass filter and watch its Bode plot, with the corner frequency fc = 1/(2πRC), the -3 dB and -45° points, and the -20 dB/decade roll-off.

    • Real-Time Processing Simulator

      Explore block size, latency, processor load and deadlines in a double-buffered real-time FIR filter running on an MCU, Cortex-M4, DSP or FPGA.

    • s-Plane Explorer

      Drag poles and zeros in the s-plane and watch the frequency response along the jω axis, the step response, the ROC, stability, and the z = e^(sT) map.

    • Sampling Theorem Explorer

      Sample a sine wave at an adjustable rate, rebuild it from its samples, and see why a tone above the Nyquist frequency folds to a lower alias frequency.

    • Signal Operations - Shift, Scale and Reverse

      Transform elementary signals by time shifting, time scaling and time reversal, and track which points of x(t) map to which points of y(t).

    • Signal Processing Timeline

      Pan and zoom a timeline of 50 milestones in the history of electronic signal processing, from the telegraph in 1837 to cloud-based processing in 2022.

    • Signal Type Explorer

      Compare continuous-time and discrete-time views of four basic signals and see how amplitude, frequency, phase, shift and scale affect energy, power and RMS.

    • Sine Wave

      Plot y(t) = A sin(2πt/T + φ) and use three sliders to see how amplitude, period and phase change a sine wave, with frequency and time-shift read-outs.

    • Sound Reverb Effect

      Play a music clip through a convolution reverb, set the decay time and the dry/wet mix, and watch the reverb tail fade after the source stops.

    • Spectral Leakage and Windowing

      Move a tone between DFT bin centres to see spectral leakage, then compare how rectangular, Hamming, Hanning, Blackman and Kaiser windows trade main-lobe width against side-lobe level.

    • Synth Timeline

      A scrollable timeline of ten landmark synthesizers, from the 1920 Theremin to the 1987 Roland D-50, in three color-coded categories.

    • System Interconnections - Series, Parallel, Feedback

      Connect gain, low-pass and delay blocks in series, parallel, negative feedback or feedforward and see the overall H(z), its poles, stability and responses.

    • System Property Explorer

      Run linearity, time-invariance, causality and BIBO stability tests on eight example systems and see which properties each system has, and why.

    • Time-Frequency Analysis Explorer

      Compute spectrograms of a chirp, an FSK signal and a note sequence, and vary the STFT window length, overlap and type to see the time-frequency trade-off.

    • Tone Generator MicroSim

      A sine-wave tone generator with a live FFT spectrum - hear the tone, set its frequency and amplitude, and watch its single spectral peak move.

    • Wavelet Explorer - CWT Scalogram and Haar DWT

      Compare a continuous wavelet transform scalogram (Morlet, Mexican hat, Haar) with a multilevel Haar DWT, and denoise a signal by hard thresholding.

    • Learning Graph Viewer V1

      Superseded first version of the learning graph viewer, with Math Foundations nodes pinned left and Applications and AI nodes pinned right.

    • Signal Processing Word Cloud

# Character Sheet: Echo the Bat

The canonical identity document for Echo, the pedagogical
mascot for the **Signal Processing with AI** textbook. Every pose prompt and every
piece of AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to
the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Echo
- **Species:** Bat
- **Subject:** Signal processing
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's tune in!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Deep maroon — hex `#800000` (matches the book's primary theme color)
- **Accent color:** Gold — hex `#E6B800` (inner ears, headphones, glasses rims), with a cream belly `#F5E6C8` and a slightly lighter maroon wing membrane `#A04545`
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small round gold-rimmed glasses and a compact pair of gold over-ear headphones worn between the ears
- **Expression:** Large, kind dark eyes behind the glasses and a gentle closed-mouth smile; curious and patient, never menacing (no visible fangs)
- **Size proportion:** Small, round, and compact — designed to read clearly at icon size (about 90 px wide)
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector cartoon with clean bold outlines and flat shading, no gradients or textures

## Personality

- Curious — always listening for the pattern hidden in the data
- Patient — normalizes confusion and repeats ideas from a new angle
- Precise — likes exact definitions, units, and clean waveforms
- Gently witty — enjoys the occasional signal pun, never at a student's expense

## Voice

- Warm and conversational, talks to students as fellow explorers of the spectrum
- Uses listening and tuning metaphors (tune in, listen closely, pick out the signal)
- Keeps it short — one to three sentences, then gets out of the way
- Signature phrases: "Let's tune in!", "Listen closely!", "Find the signal in the noise!"

## Why This Mascot

Bats navigate by echolocation — they emit a pulse, receive the echo, and extract
distance and shape from it, which is the same emit-sample-filter-analyze pipeline
this book teaches. "Echo" is also a core signal concept (delay, reverb, impulse
response) and a gender-neutral name, and the maroon-and-gold coloring ties the
character directly to the book's existing theme.
